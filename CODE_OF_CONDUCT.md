# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Focus discussion on technical facts and reproducible behavior.
- Assume good intent, but challenge ideas with evidence.
- Keep security concerns private until the maintainer confirms disclosure is safe.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's secrets, credentials, or private data.
- Deliberately disruptive spam in issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Enforcement

The repository owner may moderate discussions, close threads, or block contributors who violate these expectations.
